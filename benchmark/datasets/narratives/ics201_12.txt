**SITUATION, HEALTH, AND SAFETY (reversed order per Safety Officer's briefing format)**

Primary safety hazards for this operational period: extreme fire behavior risk from the forecast afternoon wind shift (all personnel must brief escape routes with supervisors before 13:00); snag and falling tree hazard throughout the burn scar (hard hats mandatory, chainsaw crews maintain 2-tree-length spacing); aviation conflict near drop corridors (500-foot ground exclusion zone, coordinate with Air Attack Supervisor); heat stress as temperatures rise toward 91°F (mandatory 30-minute hydration check-ins); smoke inhalation for personnel at extended exposures.

Necessary protective measures: full accountability board sign-in at each division; Tactical Channel 3 (simplex) radio mandatory; LCES briefed every morning; N95 minimum outside containment lines; SCBA inside active burn areas; dozer operators require foot crew escort within 200 feet of the fire edge.

Situation summary: Electrical arcing at the Redwood Bluff substation under red flag conditions ignited this fire on the evening of March 14, 2027 — relative humidity at 9%, offshore winds at 18 mph gusting to 34 mph, fine dead fuel moisture at 4%. Spotting across Ridgeline Road within the first 90 minutes exceeded initial attack capacity, and the incident transitioned to extended attack at 00:30 March 15. As of the morning briefing, 340 acres have burned, 12 structures are confirmed destroyed, 87 parcels remain under mandatory evacuation, and the southern and western flanks are 15% contained. The critical planning driver for this operational period is the forecast southwesterly wind shift at 14:00 that could redirect the head fire from its current northeast vector toward the Klamath River recreation corridor.

**MAP AND OPERATIONAL AREA (brief summary only — full sketch on file at ICP)**

The fire is burning within an approximately 8-by-4-mile operational footprint — roughly 32 square miles — bounded by Ridgeline Road to the north, the Klamath River corridor to the east, Pinecrest Drive to the south, and the substation access road to the west. Ignition point is Tower 14-B at the Redwood Bluff substation; initial footprint was 0.4 acres, now expanding northeast. Impacted: chaparral fuel bed on Ridgeline's southwest slope, 12 Pinecrest Drive structures destroyed, 2.1 miles of Ridgeline Road impassable. Threatened: 87-parcel Pinecrest Drive neighborhood, Redwood Bluff Volunteer Fire Station on Skyline Avenue, Klamath River watershed 1.4 miles northeast. Overflight at 04:15 confirmed crown fire on the northeast ridge with 0.6-mile ember cast; slow ground fire in the south meadow pocket. Primary spread ENE at 2.5 mph; wind shift at 14:00 may redirect toward Klamath River campground cluster. Approximately 0.8 miles of Klamath River riparian buffer have received ember deposition; no water quality impacts confirmed. Map oriented north-up using NWCG symbology; geo-referenced and loaded to ICS 204 tablets.
