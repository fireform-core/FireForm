This Safety Message and Plan (ICS 208) governs the Port of Houston Sulfuric Acid Tanker Leak operational period starting 10/14/2026 at 07:00 and concluding 10/15/2026 at 19:00.

The safety directive dictates: Primary tactical focus is placed on safe chemical neutralization and pH stabilization in the ship channel water column. Mandatory Level A chemical suit entry procedures enforced for all vessel deck operations. Due to extreme daytime heat (88°F, 78% humidity), entry work cycles are capped at 20 minutes active entry followed by 40 minutes active hydration and cooling.

A site safety plan is required for this incident (true). The approved Site Safety Plan is located at USCG Sector Houston Command Center Safety Office.
